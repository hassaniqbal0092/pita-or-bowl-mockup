How it works

Four layers. One bowl that's entirely yours.

Every order starts empty and gets built up in front of you — pick a base, a protein, whatever's fresh that day, and finish it your way.

01

Pick a base

Rice, greens, or warm pita — the foundation for everything on top.

02

Choose your protein

Chicken shawarma, falafel, gyro, or grilled veggies for a fully vegan build.

03

Load it up

Hummus, tabbouleh, pickled turnips, olives — as much as fits.

04

Finish with sauce

Garlic toum, tzatziki, or house hot sauce ties the whole bowl together.
